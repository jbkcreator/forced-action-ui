Straight answers

We reach out to property owners and investors using public property and business records. If you would rather not hear from us, say so on the call or reply STOP, and we will stop.

Are you a lender?

No. We connect investors with independent capital partners. We handle the conversation and the fit. Our partners review and approve the financing.No. We connect investors with independent capital partners, who review and approve the financing.

What kinds of properties?

Residential with one to four units: single family, townhomes and small multifamily. Flips, ground up construction and cash out after a cash purchase.

Can you move fast?
