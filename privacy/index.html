Last updated: October 14, 2026

What Information We Collect

We collect the information you give us: your name, mobile number, email address, property city, the type of project you are doing, and how many projects you have finished in the last three years. When you submit our form, we also record your IP address, browser and device information, the page address and the time, as a record of your consent. We keep records of calls and texts with you (calls are recorded) and a record of your consent to be contacted.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, to call or text you about it, to schedule calls and send confirmations and reminders, to send the weekly Deal Drop if you opted in to it, to keep records of your consent and opt-outs, and to improve our website and services. We do not sell your personal information.

How We Share Information

We share your information with capital partners only when you ask us to connect you with them, and with service providers who help us run our business (such as our texting and customer management platform), only so they can provide those services. SMS opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties for their marketing.

SMS Opt-In Details

When you tick the consent box on our form, you agree that Next Deal Lending may call and text you at the mobile number you gave about your inquiry, including with automated technology. Consent is not a condition of any financing. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

If you also tick the optional Deal Drop box, you agree to receive the Next Deal Lending weekly Deal Drop: marketing text messages with off-market deals in the areas you buy, about one message per week. Deal Drop is separate from the texts about your inquiry and is not required to submit our form. Reply STOP to opt out.

No Sharing of SMS Opt-Ins

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising tracking. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser information when a page loads, as described in Google's privacy policy.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss or misuse. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights, Including How to Unsubscribe or Update Your Information

  • To stop text messages, reply STOP to any message.
  • To stop calls or emails, tell us and we will stop contacting you.
  • To see, update or delete the information we hold about you, contact us at hello@nextdeallending.com, or call or text us at (656) 221-1100.
