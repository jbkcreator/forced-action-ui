SMS Program Description

Next Deal Lending's text messaging program sends messages about your financing inquiry. These include replies to your questions, a follow-up text if we miss your call, and call confirmations and reminders. You join by ticking the text message box on our form or by texting us first. Consent is not a condition of obtaining any financing. Messages are sent using automated technology.

Call Consent

Calls have their own separate box on our form. If you tick it, you agree that Next Deal Lending may call you at the number you gave about your inquiry, including with automated technology. Calls are recorded. Consent is not a condition of obtaining any financing. To stop calls, tell us on any call or email optout@nextdeallending.com.

Eligibility

You must be 18 years of age or older to use this SMS service.

Deal Drop (Optional Weekly Email)

If you also tick the optional Deal Drop box on our form, you will receive the Next Deal Lending weekly Deal Drop by email, with off-market deals in the areas you buy, about one email per week. Deal Drop is sent by email only, not by text message. It is separate from the texts and calls about your inquiry and is not required to submit our form. Unsubscribe at any time with the link in any email or by emailing optout@nextdeallending.com.

Message Frequency, Message and Data Rates

Text message frequency varies depending on your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

How to Opt Out (STOP) and Get Help (HELP)

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You may also reply STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT. You will receive one confirmation message and then no further texts. Reply HELP for help. To receive texts again after opting out, reply START or submit our form again.

Carrier Disclaimer

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Support Contact

For questions or help, reply HELP to any text, call or text us at (656) 221-1100, or email hello@nextdeallending.com.

Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information is described in our Privacy Policy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
